# Users of StaySpot

## 1. Guest (traveller)

Someone who books holiday stays and pays from a prepaid wallet.

What they want to do:
- Find a property and see its price per night.
- Book a stay for chosen dates and immediately see how much was taken from the wallet.
- Understand *why* a booking failed (not enough money, wrong dates) without reading a database error.
- Top up the wallet.
- Check in to a stay, and later mark it completed.
- Look back at every wallet change (wallet statement).

Screens they use: **Browse**, **Book a stay**, **Wallet audit**, **Reviews** (to read about a property).

## 2. Operations / revenue analyst (StaySpot staff)

Someone who watches how the business is doing and where demand is.

What they want to do:
- See headline numbers (revenue, stays, nights) and know how fresh they are.
- Refresh the numbers on demand.
- See revenue trends for one property and which properties are performing best right now.
- See where guests are searching *right now* on a map, to spot demand hotspots.
- See what guests say in reviews: rating spread, most common tags, average rating.
- Look up any guest, property or booking.

Screens they use: **Analytics**, **Search map**, **Reviews**, **Browse**.

## Demo actor switch

There is no login. A "Switch" button in the navbar lets the presenter choose which guest they are acting as
(saved in the browser's localStorage). All guest screens use that guest.
